## 1. Cross-cutting decisions (the themes the five reviews converged on)

1. **Emergent, not authored** (GL-01/CT-20 held everywhere). No authored story POIs, puzzles,
   set-piece towers or guaranteed rooms. Where the reviews wanted a guarantee they chose a
   *generation bias or post-roll check*, not authored content: one deadbolted pocket door on the
   spawn tower's dry floors (S1-07), one `helmet_lamp` in a neighbouring dry pocket (S1-16), a
   `LootGen` post-roll pass that forces `schematic_hard_suit` / `schematic_rebreather` into a safe if
   the seed rolled none (S4-33), a 100 % pocket roll on the row 400–440 floor of one tower per
   district cluster (S4-49), a stone-object density target on submerged floors (S2-14), an iron-per-
   world assertion ≥ 2× the gear chain (S3-18). Each gets a gate assertion.
2. **Presentation over new systems.** Most "how does the player read X" questions were answered
   with HUD/audio/VFX that hang off existing state: HUD depth/band readout + cold indicator
   (S2-05, S2-28, S3-28, S4-20, S4-28), O2 turnaround tick at 50 % (S2-05), skill-up toast on the gain
   feed (S1-26), carried-weight readout (S1-08), pump loop SFX + outlet bubbles + room-dry stinger
   (S2-11), tide-line decal on drained rooms (S2-22), breach drift particles that stop when a cell
   is sealed (S2-39), inrush notice (S2-42), container glint until looted (S2-18), lock-tier shield
   on hover cards + lock markers on the map (S3-08/17/25), Drowned eyeshine + aggro call (S4-21/37),
   frost vignette + grace before cold DPS (S4-20), hull groan + crush fringe (S4-28/48).
3. **No new meters, durability, or skill lines.** No chill accumulation (S3-10), no light budget
   (S4-06), no suit maintenance (S4-12), no seepage or pressure leaks (S2-10, S4-23/39, S5-40), no
   fourth skill in MVP (S1-27, S2-27, S3-26), no per-level skill perks (S2-25), no respec (S4-26),
   no post-tree prestige (S5-25). The 3×3 ability tree stays the finite goal.
4. **Water canon reaffirmed.** "Drained stays dry" is a promise (S2-09, S4-39). Uniform water,
   one pump tier, `PUMP_RANGE_BLOCKS` 48 the only constraint (S2-32, S3-41, S5-40). Fill-to-drain vs
   patch-and-pump pricing already works (S2-40). Bucket is the hand-carry answer, no barrels (S2-23).
   The drained elevator shaft is the mid-stage goal of Stage 3 and the advertised MVP mega-project
   of Stage 5 (S3-09, S5-09); a pump-outlet current up a dry shaft is the endgame's last lesson
   (S5-11/49). A whole-tower drain is not advertised until the M6 perf pass measures it (S5-41).
5. **Depletion / GL-28 held.** Surface iron stays at zero (S1-30); the wetsuit stays a **scrap-tier**
   Stage 2 craft so The Cold is never locked behind its own loot (S2-15, GL-11 amendment); iron
   concentrates in the industrial cluster as "the mine" (S3-03/18); Crush loot is denser, not richer
   (S5-17); no modifier manuals or rerolls (S5-14).
6. **Stage boundaries stay capability checklists, not gates.** Stage 1→2 requires only the tank
   (S1-43/44); Stage 2→3 only the wetsuit (S2-43), with a Shallows camp nudged by O2 arithmetic
   (S2-44); Stage 3→4 is a steel budget, torch first (S3-29/48); Stage 4→5 is the hard suit alone,
   descending on the iron tank (S4-45/50); Stage 5 completion = feet on the ground row inside
   `city_bounds` with crush rating ≥ 1 → world flag + summit card, no credits (S5-28/43/44).
7. **Roof-era start absorbed.** The medical room is gone from every doc: spawn = the centre tower's
   roof, first tool = `wood_axe`, scrap tools are Workbench recipes at 5x cost, the roof is the
   ideal first base and the wood farm for the whole run (S1-02/05/06/24/49, S2-49).

## 2. Conflicts between agents, and how they were reconciled

- **Crush fringe.** Stage 5 (S5-31) first said "no damaging fringe"; Stage 4 (S4-48) proposed a 6-row
  ramp 5 → 25 HP/s below row 440. Adopted **S4-48**: the wall stays absolute (only crush rating
  stops it) but its first half-floor is retreatable. S5-31 rewritten to point at S4-48.
- **Elevator cab.** Stage 2 (S2-03) makes it a static pry-tier-1 object of the `roof_hatch` class;
  Stage 3 (S3-07) wanted a chained (lock 2) cab door. Reconciled: the Shallows cab is tier 1, a
  chained variant exists for Cold-band shafts, the movable cab is deferred (S2-37).
- **HUD depth readout gating.** S2-05 wants it always-on for new divers; S4-28 suggested gating it
  behind the dive watch / hard suit. Kept always-on per S2-05; gating is now open topic S4-54
  (and S3-52 for the cold indicator).
- **Steel budget.** Three agents quoted the docs' pre-5x costs (torch 2 steel, cutters 3 iron,
  "18 iron + 9 stone"). `data/recipes.json` has torch **10 steel + 15 scrap + 10 plastic**, bolt
  cutters and iron knife **15 iron + 5 wood**. All tables and the chain text were corrected to
  **17 steel = 34 iron + 17 stone (+15 iron cutters)**. Whether the Cold's iron budget still covers
  that is a real balance question — logged as **S3-56** for the user.
